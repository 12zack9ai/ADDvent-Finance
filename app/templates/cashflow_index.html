Weekly run-rates — what continues whether or not a bill is on file

Payroll, insurance and rent don't stop because nobody has entered a bill yet. Any week with no real bill in a category uses these instead. A week with a real bill uses the real one. Leave blank to use nothing. Without these, the back half of the forecast reads as costing nothing.

Subcontractor and supplier payments get no run-rate: they are job-driven, and inventing a weekly figure would be fabricating cost.
